Create one original, refined geometric symbol. A bold cobalt-blue magnifying lens whose central circular lens encloses two parallel code/document columns, with one short cyan horizontal bar bridging or aligning the two columns, subtly communicating side-by-side code comparison. Integrate the diagonal handle cleanly into the silhouette. Clear large negative spaces, thick confident shapes, very few details. Use cobalt #2563EB and dark navy #153A8A with a small cyan #22D3EE accent; flat vector-like fills, crisp edges, balanced optical weight.
Square composition, one centered symbol filling about 84% of the canvas, generous consistent transparent padding, actual transparent alpha background. Designed to remain legible at 24px and 32px, professional developer utility aesthetic. No lettering, no words, no ParitySQL text, no numbers, no watermark, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no extra variants, no outer app tile or square background, no shadows, no glow, no photographic texture, no gradients. Deliver a single high-resolution square transparent PNG logo.
